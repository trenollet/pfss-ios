# PFSS Product Vision

- Status: Active
- Owner: PFSS Project
- Applies To: v0.9+
- Last Updated: 2026-07-18

## Product Identity

**Product Name:** Pro Field Sales and Service  
**Short Name:** PFSS  
**Product Type:** Mobile-first field-service sales and operations platform

PFSS moves a customer from first contact through completed work and final payment with as little friction as possible.

## Mission

Build an affordable, adaptable field-service platform whose primary workplace is the phone. Industry-specific applications may change terminology, service catalog content, branding, and workflow emphasis while preserving the underlying business architecture.

## Core Workflow

```text
Lead
  ↓
Customer
  ↓
Site
  ↓
Estimate
  ↓
Job
  ↓
Invoice
  ↓
Payment
  ↓
Receipt
```

PFSS is designed around workflows rather than static records. Every screen should help the user understand and complete the next logical action.

## Product Goal

Optimize for time-to-completion, not feature count.

A feature belongs in PFSS when it helps someone complete meaningful field-service work faster, with fewer taps, less duplicate entry, and less confusion.

## Experience Direction

The data belongs to the business. The experience belongs to the person using it.

PFSS should adapt the visible experience based on role, persona, workflow state, context, and industry while preserving one shared set of durable business data.

## Core Personas

### Sales
Leads, customers, sites, estimates, follow-up, and closing work.

### Technician
Today's jobs, navigation, customer contact, adding work, photos, signatures, payments, receipts, and completion.

### Office
Scheduling, customer management, estimates, invoices, payments, and operational follow-up.

### Owner
Business performance, reporting, revenue, users, configuration, catalog management, and strategy.

## Product Pillars

- Workflow-first navigation
- Technician-first mobile usability
- WorkOrder Engine
- Service Catalog
- Pricing Engine
- Catalog Ranking and recommendations
- Persona-driven experience
- Explainable business engines
- Offline-capable operation where practical
- Reusable architecture that supports multiple service industries

## Platform Direction

PFSS should feel less like a database and more like an operational assistant. Future desktop and web access should use the same durable records and reusable business engines.
