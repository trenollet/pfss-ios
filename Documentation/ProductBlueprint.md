# PFSS Product Blueprint

## Product Identity

**Product Name:** Pro Field Sales and Service  
**Short Name:** PFSS  
**Product Type:** Field service sales and operations platform

PFSS is built to solve a specific business need in the field service industry: move a customer from first contact through completed work and final payment with as little friction as possible.

PFSS is not designed to do everything under the sun. It is designed around a durable workflow that can be adapted across industries by changing labels, service catalog content, and business terminology while preserving the underlying architecture.

## Core Vision

PFSS is not designed around records. It is designed around workflows.

The platform should help a field service company manage the complete lifecycle:

```text
Lead
  ↓
Customer
  ↓
Site
  ↓
Estimate
  ↓
Job
  ↓
Invoice
  ↓
Payment
  ↓
Receipt
```

## Product Goal

Optimize for time-to-completion, not feature count.

A feature belongs in PFSS when it helps a user complete meaningful work faster, with fewer taps, less duplicate entry, and less confusion.

## Experience Principle

The data belongs to the business. The experience belongs to the person using it.

PFSS should adapt the visible experience based on persona and workflow state while preserving one shared set of business data.

## Core Personas

### Sales Persona
Focused on leads, customers, sites, estimates, follow-up, and closing work.

### Technician Persona
Focused on today's jobs, navigation, customer contact, adding work, photos, signatures, payments, receipts, and completion.

### Office Persona
Focused on scheduling, customer management, estimates, invoices, payments, and operational follow-up.

### Owner Persona
Focused on business performance, reporting, revenue, users, configuration, catalog management, and strategy.

## Platform Direction

PFSS should feel less like a database and more like an operational assistant.

The platform should surface the next logical action rather than forcing users to hunt through screens.

## Current Product Pillars

- Workflow-first navigation
- WorkOrder Engine
- Service Catalog
- Pricing Engine
- Catalog Ranking Engine
- Persona-driven experience
- Explainable business engines
