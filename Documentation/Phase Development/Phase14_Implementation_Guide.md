## Step Definitions
### Step 1 – Assignment Engine
Outcome: Establish the Assignment as the core operational object with lifecycle, scheduling modes, ownership, crew support and history.

### Step 2 – Dispatch Engine
Outcome: Operational assignment, reassignment, emergency insertion and status management.

### Step 3 – Daily Planner Engine
Outcome: Automatically build optimized technician workdays.

### Step 4 – Route Engine Integration
Outcome: Optimize travel while respecting scheduling constraints.

### Step 5 – Workforce Intelligence
Outcome: Operational employee profiles that power intelligent recommendations.

### Step 6 – Recommendation Engine
Outcome: PFSS recommends; humans decide.

### Step 7 – Dispatch Board
Outcome: Technician-centric operations dashboard.

### Step 8 – Timeline View
Outcome: Time-based operational visualization.

### Step 9 – Live Map View
Outcome: Geographic operational awareness.

### Step 10 – Field Operations
Outcome: Complete technician workflow.

### Step 11 – Operational Analytics
Outcome: Intelligence dataset for future optimization.

### Step 12 – Stabilization
Outcome: Production-ready Phase 14.
